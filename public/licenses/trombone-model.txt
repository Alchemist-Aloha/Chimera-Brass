ChimeraBrass tenor trombone model and schematic

Published dimensional reference:
Yamaha YSL-354 specifications: Bb, 12.7 mm / 0.500 inch bore, 204.4 mm / 8 inch bell.
https://ca.yamaha.com/en/musical-instruments/brass-woodwinds/products/trombones/ysl-354/specs.html

These dimensions inform a generic straight-tenor prototype, not a reverse-engineered or validated Yamaha instrument. The 2.976 m path, internal cup/backbore/leadpipe taper, 650 mm flare (chosen so computed resonances follow a B♭ partial series), loss, effective lip width/mass and travel speed are modelling assumptions. No Yamaha trombone photograph, engraving or software is redistributed. No manufacturer affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Hand travel adds twice its distance to the air path. Nominal position lengths are references; actual sound emerges from pressure, the Chimera-derived head, a self-oscillating lip valve and the bidirectional bore. The fractional-delay hand slide preserves waves but does not solve full moving-wall fluid mechanics. The model has no F attachment. Trumpet MIDI presets are not used for trombone.
