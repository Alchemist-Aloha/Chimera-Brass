ODE TO JOY — Ludwig van Beethoven (1770–1827)
Mutopia Project, Peter Chubb, Mutopia-2009/08/05-528.
The typesetter explicitly placed this edition in the public domain.
Edition: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=528
Score: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BeethovenLv/ode/ode-a4.pdf
MIDI: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BeethovenLv/ode/ode.mid
LilyPond: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BeethovenLv/ode/ode.ly
Original MIDI and LilyPond retained in third_party/scores/ode-mutopia.*.
SHA-256 MIDI: fb1604c08c865b275b464d74e5a7c526ff1a8acccdf9853cb92f0778daaed14b
SHA-256 LilyPond: a0c865aa9f71221605362622d281405071e0734540e86ce131131757cc803a71

Adaptation: the first eight bars of the soprano melody are extracted from the
upper staff of the SATB MIDI; accompaniment and the remaining eight bars are
omitted. Original G-major pitches and dotted-quarter/eighth-note cadences are
retained. This is G4–D5 concert, five semitones below our previous C5–G5 demo.
Tempo is reduced from 100 to 80 quarter notes/minute for the physical model.

WILLIAM TELL OVERTURE — Gioachino Rossini (1792–1868), 1829
The underlying composition is public domain. This is a new, monophonic MIDI
transcription of the opening gallop theme only, not the full overture or its
orchestration. No modern engraved page, accompaniment or recording is bundled.
Melody/rhythm cross-check: ABC transcription, credited there as
"Rossini arr Hugh Barwell, via PR", mirrored by John Chambers:
https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/Contra/march/William_Tell_Overture-C--/0000
Alternative mirror of the same notation:
https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/mirror/LesterBailey/melnets_big_abc_file/15528
Historical full-score catalogue (Breitkopf & Härtel, 1899, public domain):
https://imslp.org/wiki/Guillaume_Tell_(Rossini,_Gioacchino)
The historical PDF could not be retrieved automatically (IMSLP bot check).
8notes prohibits digital redistribution of its edition, so it is NOT bundled.

Adaptation: first theme only (39 notes), no repeats; two eighth-note pickups,
quarter/eighth gallop rhythm and the tied 2.5-beat high note retained. Transposed
from the ABC reference's C major to D major (A4–A5 concert), rather than the
orchestral E major, to stay within the model's supported range. Slow practice
tempo: 71 quarter notes/minute, not the orchestral Presto.
